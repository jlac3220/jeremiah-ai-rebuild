# OG.1 — Contextual visual production sheet

**State:** Production brief, not an approved image set. **Teaching content and standards remain unchanged.**

## Editorial rule

Read the exact teaching statement and its KJV passage, then describe **what is happening** before creating or sourcing artwork. A named person is not enough. A labeled image is not evidence that it depicts the cited event. Each chosen asset belongs to a stable `standardId + moveId + moment + contextRevision`; no runtime image discovery or cycling through alternatives. Review any standard, teaching, or visual replacement before republishing.

The visual baseline established by the October 30 Moses concept is **sharp, cinematic, detailed, believable ancient-world reconstruction** (not watercolor, low-detail haze, interchangeable robed men, or gratuitous crowds). This is a stylistic reference, **not a historical portrait**. No images of God as a human figure. Use genuine historical photographs for actual artifacts, authentic contemporary photography for modern life, and typography/interactivity for doctrinal comparisons.

## Asset review gate

Each proposed file must carry:
- `standardId`, `moveId`, `momentId`, `passage`, and a *verbatim or faithful compact paraphrase* of the teaching statement.
- `subject`, `action`, `timePeriod`, `setting`, `imageType`, `visualBrief`, `assetId`, `rightsStatus`, `editorialStatus`, and `contentRevision`.
- The image must pass **subject match**, **action match**, **scripture match**, **period/place plausibility**, **consistent quality**, **mobile legibility**, and **rights** review.
- If the intended subject, action, or reference is not confidently represented, mark `needs-art`. Never imply that an unrelated painting depicts the referenced verse.
- Generate or source candidates during **authoring**, download/store approved image bytes in the project's durable media storage, and bind the exact approved asset. External search result URLs and generated previews are not durable app assets.
- Existing images from unrelated passages—Moses at a different event, Isaiah holding scarlet wool (Isaiah 1), and Paul with Barnabas in Antioch (Acts 11)—must not be accepted as depictions of Deuteronomy 6:4, Isaiah 44:6, or 1 Corinthians 8:4.
- Before declaring this sheet implemented, verify asset files and perform a visual walkthrough of every listed OG.1 surface in desktop **and mobile** views. Production rights and final approval remain outstanding.

**Out of scope:** Incidental background audio about a Jewish child's first prayer is not authored OG.1 content and must not become a new lesson scene without a separately approved text change.
