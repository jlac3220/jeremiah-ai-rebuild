- **65 standards** have at least one plausible topic-specific lead; **60 still need another source or a purpose-built activity.**

- Existing content, assessments and doctrinal standards have **not** been changed.

These numbers represent *potential context matches*, not educational quality, doctrinal alignment, source permissions or classroom readiness.

## Sample educational experiences to develop

1. **NB.1:** Pair the verified *Khata / Sin* publisher page with a **Fall → Separation → Need** interactive cause/effect flow. The Bible passage establishes doctrine; the video is word-study context.
2. **NB.2:** Use *Covenants*, *What Is Passover?* and *Sacrifice and Atonement* as alternative background resources alongside a clickable redemptive-history timeline. Avoid playing all three consecutively.
3. **OG.4:** Build a tappable tabernacle floor plan linked to Exodus 19–40, plus the *Temple* background video. The video does not establish Apostolic doctrine on its own.
4. **NB.7 / OG.12:** Use the officially published *Pentecost: Acts 1–7* overview to orient the learner, then show an **Acts 2 sequence** built from Scripture itself.
5. **NB.8:** Use an Acts account comparison matrix that tracks preaching, response, water baptism and Spirit reception; use *Acts 1–12* / *Acts 13–28* only for broad historical narrative.
6. **OG.7 / OG.8:** Use *John 1* and the *Colossians* overview **only for textual/literary context**. Jeremiah must supply the actual Apostolic interpretation of Colossians 2:9 and the incarnation.
7. **NB.5 / NB.10:** Prioritize a **Scripture-based baptism and name-of-Jesus comparison**. An external video has not yet been validated as suitable for the central apostolic doctrinal claims.
8. **OG.14:** Build a chronology using accurately dated, attributed church-history primary documents. Do not assert that a museum artifact or a generic video proves a disputed historical narrative.

## Nonnegotiable review boundaries

- **Publisher listing verified ≠ complete spoken-content review.** No video is assigned a clip time or declared doctrinally approved solely from its title, overview, transcript availability or publisher reputation.
- **External media supplements Scripture, never replaces the Apostolic standard.** Correct false or competing readings explicitly in Jeremiah’s teaching.
- **BibleProject rights:** [App embedding guidance](https://help.bibleproject.com/hc/en-us/articles/22547623073687-I-m-building-an-app-Can-I-use-your-content-in-it) and [Terms](https://bibleproject.com/terms/) allow eligible embeds of **unaltered full videos** in non-paywalled/noncommercial contexts with prominent publisher credit. They do **not** generally authorize clipped/re-edited/rehosted excerpts or charging users to access that media. Validate Jeremiah’s intended monetization and embedding method before implementation.
- **Museum imagery:** verify the **specific object** is marked public domain, with object-specific credit. An Old Babylonian tablet or Roman lamp is context, **not** a first-century Christian artifact or proof of any biblical event.
- **Do not auto-publish.** Actual lesson placement requires reviewing content, doctrine, learner usefulness, platform access, display behavior on iPhone and publisher rights.
