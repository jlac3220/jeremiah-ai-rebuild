## 6. Assessment and evidence specification

- **Low-stakes diagnostic:** Does not skip required instruction.
- **Formative checks:** Specific feedback; retries allowed.
- **Independent exit evidence:** Must test what was taught without leading the learner.
- **Assessment bank:** Question, expected evidence, distractor rationale (if any), explanation, standard ID, DOK, standard-evidence level.
- **Rubric:** Observable criteria for levels 1–4 (aligned to source, not merely a percentage).
- **Progression/retake:** What occurs after partial understanding, and how feedback supports retry.
- **Mapping to larger lesson/domain exam:** Specify partial coverage and do not overclaim mastery.

## 7. Visual, narration, and accessibility design

- **Visual scenes:** Character, action, place, era, and reason each scene belongs here.
- **Audio:** A single coherent narrator, short optional autoplay, full read/text controls.
- **Interaction variety:** Interaction chosen for the cognitive job.
- **Accessibility:** Captions, keyboard, reduced motion, contrast, readable mobile layout, alternate text.
- **Media verification:** Sources, licenses, claims, and fallback assets.
